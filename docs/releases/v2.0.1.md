# Local Dictation 2.0.1

[Download Local-Dictation.dmg](https://github.com/ysham123/imnotpayingforwisprflow/releases/download/v2.0.1/Local-Dictation.dmg).
Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 14 or newer, English dictation.

Local Dictation 2.0 adds more control over how you record and what happens to
your words. Dictation remains on-device, and the app still needs no account,
subscription, or API key.

## What's new

- **Choose how your transcript reads.** Clean mode removes fillers and handles
  clear spoken corrections. Verbatim mode skips the separate cleanup model.
- **Record longer thoughts.** Dictate for up to five minutes, with a live
  elapsed timer, microphone-level meter, and final countdown. Long pauses no
  longer end a recording early.
- **Choose your microphone.** Select and test an input device in Settings. If a
  saved device is unavailable, the app explains that it has switched to the
  system default.
- **Recover without repeating yourself.** If speech recognition fails, retry
  from the recording held in memory or discard it. Cleanup can recover in the
  background while preserving the original transcript.
- **Find controls in native Settings.** General, Audio, Shortcuts, Custom Words,
  and Advanced settings are grouped in one window. Opening Settings no longer
  gets in the way of an active dictation session.
- **Return text to where you started.** 2.0.1 can restore the
  original field and caret after you switch apps or move the cursor, when the
  original content is unchanged and the editor supports Accessibility focus
  and selection. If it cannot verify the destination, it holds the text for
  explicit placement instead of guessing.

## Privacy

Speech recognition and cleanup use local models. Audio normally stays in
memory; one failed recording can remain there until retry, discard, or quit.
There is no transcript history or cloud transcription fallback. Existing model
weights, shortcuts, and saved words remain local across compatible updates.

## Updating

Finish your dictation and quit Local Dictation. Open the DMG, open
Local Dictation.app, and choose **Install & Open**. Existing verified models,
shortcuts, and custom words are reused.

The public app is ad-hoc signed and is not notarized by Apple. macOS may require
**Privacy & Security → Open Anyway** on first launch and renewed Microphone,
Accessibility, or Input Monitoring approval after this update. The creator's
optional local certificate is separate from public distribution; its restart
results do not establish permission continuity for the public package.

## Validation and known limits

The production build and automated macOS regression suites pass. The public
DMG is checked for integrity, signed-bundle contents, and installation into an
isolated destination. See [the validation record](../VALIDATION.md) for details.

Live placement across multiple third-party editors, repeated restarts, fresh-Mac
installation, and permission continuity across a later public update remain
unverified. An unsupported or changed editor keeps your transcript available
for explicit placement or Copy. Synthetic model measurements describe the
tested samples; they are not a general accuracy guarantee.

Bring-your-own model connections, browser search, and coding-agent sessions are
proposed work in [the product roadmap](../PRODUCT-ROADMAP.md).
